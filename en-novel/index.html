《Your Novel Title Here》

Synopsis:
I wanted to write an otherworldly story that differs from traditional styles. I hope you enjoy it.

"㞸", pronounced as *Sui*.
Everything in this world possesses 㞸 (*Sui*).
Huyang just took a nap, but when he woke up, he had become a seven or eight-year-old child by the river.
No oracles, no missions, and no desire to save the world.
A white-haired girl poked him awake with a branch and asked if he could be her friend.
That afternoon, sitting by the river, he used a branch to teach her how to write one, two, and three in the mud.
He didn't know why he was doing it.
Maybe just out of boredom.
Later, the white-haired girl became a magical genius revered by the world. The bells of the Holy City gradually lost their rhythm, oracles fell silent, nations collapsed, and demons crossed the borders.
Many spread their wings to rise, leaving their own shadows under the moon of 㞸 (*Sui*).
This is a story about a stranger from another world, a white-haired girl, a holy city, a princess, oracles, and exiles.
It is also the *Heying* (the shadow of wings and shared reflection) of their time existing together.
